Quality expectations should be clear before production begins. We encourage buyers to share their target market, fabric standards, measurements, tolerances, construction details, testing requirements and finishing expectations.

For each project, the product brief should define the approved sample, measurement chart, material and trim specifications, labelling, packaging and inspection requirements. Any testing or compliance documents are shared only where they are applicable, current and verified.
