MANISHA OVERSEAS — OPEN THE WEBSITE
==================================

1. Download the ZIP file and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted folder named Manisha-Overseas-Website.
3. Double-click index.html. This is the homepage.
4. Use the top navigation or click All Pages to browse the site.
5. Choose 3D Dress Studio in the top navigation to see the revolving garment.

MAIN PAGE ORDER
1. Home — reference-matched editorial homepage
2. 3D Dress Studio — revolving 3D dress concept; drag to inspect and use pause/reset
3. About Us — company story and manufacturing identity
4. Collections — seven category pages and 21 illustrative product pages
5. Fabrics & Craftsmanship — deeper look at cotton, Schiffli, embroidery and prints
6. Buyer Process — how wholesale enquiries move from brief to production planning
7. Manufacturing, Private Label, Wholesale, Quality and Contact

TROUBLESHOOTING
- Keep an internet connection on for Google Fonts and Three.js.
- The dress studio includes a rotating illustrated fallback if WebGL/Three.js cannot load.
- If Windows blocks local scripts, open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
- Do not open individual HTML pages from inside the ZIP without extracting the full folder first.
