MANISHA OVERSEAS / PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

From a product idea
to a clear brief.

We work with apparel brands and wholesale buyers to review style references, fabric direction, surface detail, sizing, sampling needs and the commercial information required for a focused manufacturing conversation.

01 / THE DEVELOPMENT ROUTE

Six steps to a
buyer-ready specification.

This is a working framework. The exact activities, timing, sample scope and commercial terms are agreed for each project.

01

Share your reference

Sample, sketch, mood board, product photo or technical pack. Include target market, category and quantity where known.

02

Review fabric and surface

Preferred fabric, weight, hand-feel, print, embroidery, Schiffli cutwork, eyelet placement, trims and colour direction.

03

Clarify fit and construction

Silhouette, size range, measurements, construction details, finishing, labels and garment requirements.

04

Review sampling needs

Where sampling is agreed, define sample type, reference standard, review points and changes before production approval.

05

Confirm commercial details

Quantity, colour split, target costing, packaging, labelling, destination requirements, lead-time expectations and trade terms.

06

Plan production and dispatch

Once product specifications and commercial terms are agreed, align production planning, inspection expectations, packing and shipment arrangements.

02 / WHAT TO INCLUDE

A useful brief
makes the next step clearer.

You do not need every answer at first contact. Share what is available; the team can identify which details are still needed.

ProductCategory, style references, sketches or tech pack
FabricComposition, weight, print, embroidery or surface treatment
FitSize range, measurement chart, fit notes and construction
OrderTarget quantity, colours, size ratio and repeat potential
CommercialTarget price direction, packaging, labels and trade terms
DestinationCountry, delivery window and buyer requirements
03 / IMPORTANT TO ALIGN EARLY

MOQ, price and lead time
depend on the product brief.

Manisha Overseas does not publish one universal MOQ, price or lead time for every category. These depend on material, design complexity, colour and size breakdown, order quantity, sampling scope and destination. They are confirmed after review.
