Every order begins with the product details. This outline helps buyers prepare a focused enquiry and gives the team the information needed to review it.

01

Share the brief

Send reference images, a tech pack, category, target market and any essential construction or finishing details.

02

Review fabric & feasibility

Discuss fabric direction, surface detail, fit, size range, colour options and questions that affect product development.

03

Align commercial details

Review quantities, destination market, target pricing, packaging requirements and information needed for a clear quotation.

04

Confirm sample & specification

Where sampling is agreed, align on the reference sample, measurements, materials, trims and quality expectations before production.

05

Plan production & shipment

Once specifications and commercial terms are confirmed, align the production plan and shipping requirements.
