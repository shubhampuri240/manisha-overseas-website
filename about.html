03 / OUR STORY

Made in India.
Made for the world.

Manisha Overseas is a direct manufacturer and exporter of women's western apparel, working with cotton, prints, embroidery and Schiffli cutwork for wholesale buyers and private labels.

WHO WE ARE

A manufacturing partner, not a middleman.

Based in Noida, India, Manisha Overseas develops and manufactures women's western wear across dresses, tops, skirts, pants, coordinated sets and resort styles.

Our work brings together fabric selection, garment construction and finishing, with a particular focus on cotton and Schiffli cutwork styles when the design calls for them.
