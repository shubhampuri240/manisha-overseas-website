01 / WHO WE ARE

A manufacturing partner.
Not a middleman.

Manisha Overseas works directly in women's apparel manufacturing and export from Noida, India. The business is focused on developing and producing garments—not simply passing buyer orders to an unrelated supplier.

Product conversations can begin with a finished reference, buyer mood board, sample or technical pack. The discussion is shaped around the garment: fabric choice, print or embroidery, silhouette, fit, construction, finish, quantity and destination market.

We work with buyers to understand what a collection needs and establish product details before sampling, costing and production planning.

03 / FABRIC & SURFACE

Details that give
a garment its character.

Cotton is central to many product directions. Depending on the design and buyer brief, development may also consider selected rayon or nylon fabrics, prints, embroidery, eyelet and Schiffli cutwork. Surface work is part of the whole garment—its placement, scale and finishing matter.

Schiffli is relevant when a style calls for openwork, eyelet motifs or embroidered cotton detail. We do not apply the term to every lace or embroidered material.
