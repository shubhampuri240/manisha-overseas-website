## Rules for adding external product photographs

1. Match each candidate photo against Manisha's own sample, style code, shipment records or production catalogue.
2. Verify the seller account belongs to the relevant partner. Do not assume any Red Rose shop is the same legal customer.
3. Secure permission from the copyright owner or use an original Manisha-owned, commercially approved photograph.
4. Do not upload photos of unrelated designs, third-party models or retail customers as Manisha's manufacturing portfolio.
5. If confirmed, save the highest-quality original file to a dedicated per-style folder, with source, owner, usage rights, date, style code, and confirmed status.
6. Do not automatically scrape VK, Instagram, WhatsApp or Telegram or bypass access controls.
7. A single front photo is only enough for photo-based texture *study*, never a verified 360° scan. Better 3D requires side/back photographs, tech packs and/or a 3D CAD model.
